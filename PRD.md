# RentEase – Furniture & Appliance Rental Platform

## Purpose

RentEase helps students and working professionals rent essential furniture and appliances on flexible monthly plans. It reduces upfront purchase costs, relocation effort, and maintenance concerns.

## In scope

- Responsive web platform for furniture and appliance rentals
- Product catalog, tenure plans, security deposits, cart, and checkout
- Delivery and pickup scheduling
- Active-rental management, extension, return history, and maintenance support
- Admin inventory, users, rentals, damage reports, service areas, and analytics

## Out of scope

- Native mobile applications
- Cross-border rentals
- Advanced AI pricing
- Second-hand resale marketplace

## Functional requirements

### Customer

- Register and log in
- Browse furniture and appliances by category
- View product pricing, deposit, stock, and tenure options
- Add items to cart and schedule delivery
- View active rentals and rental history
- Request extension, pickup, repair, and damage support

### Admin

- Manage products, prices, tenure options, and inventory
- Monitor orders, pickups, returns, and product availability
- Resolve maintenance and damage requests
- View registered users, service areas, and KPI analytics

## KPIs

- Active rentals
- Monthly recurring revenue (MRR)
- Product utilization
- Customer retention rate
- Average maintenance-resolution time

## Technology

- Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript
- Backend: Node.js and Express
- Database: SQLite
- Authentication: JWT and bcrypt password hashing

## Deliverables

- Functional full-stack web application
- Admin dashboard
- PRD and technical documentation
- Deployment-ready configuration
